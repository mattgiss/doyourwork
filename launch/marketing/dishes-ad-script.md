# "The Dishes" — 65-second animated ad

Soft, rounded, flat animation (think meditation-app explainer). Narrated with ElevenLabs: Cecily (narrator), Chris (Dad), Tiffany (Mom). Record again with `cd tools/voiceover && npm run record`.
Works with sound off: every line is on screen.

| Time | Picture | Sound and words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7 s | Morning. Dad at the bathroom mirror, brushing his teeth, foam everywhere. He leans out the door. | Brushing. Dad, mouth full: "Wan' coffee, hon?" Mom, from the bedroom: "Yes please!" |
| 7–15 s | Kitchen. Dad shuffles in with a mug and stops dead. The sink is a tower of dishes, a frying pan on top, a fly circling. His face drops. He opens his mouth… a car zooms past the window. | A car horn honks right over the swear word. The bubble reads "#@%&!" |
| 15–22 s | Freeze. Split screen: Dad on one side, a kid on the other, both shrugging. | Narrator: "Every house has the dishes talk. Parents, say it once. Kids, stop getting asked to do what you were already gonna do." |
| 22–32 s | One tablet on the counter. Four character tiles: Dad the bear, Mom the fox, the kids the otter and the dragon. Each taps their tile and fills a card of chores up to 25 minutes, then sends it. | Narrator: "Meet DOYOURWORK. Everyone picks today's chores on one phone or tablet. A parent approves. Points go up." |
| 32–42 s | A bowling-style scoreboard. Each finished chore knocks down pins. Three steps appear. | Narrator: "It's as simple as going bowling. Download the app. Put in a name for everyone. Then play: do the chores already in there, or add your own." |
| 42–48 s | Plum, after-dark colors. Phones in hands, sealed cards. | Narrator: "Got your own phones? Dare each other. Guess which chores someone will do. It's revealed after midnight." |
| 48–55 s | Back in the kitchen: the sink sparkles, Dad hands Mom her coffee, the otter wins the week with a crown. End card. | Narrator: "DOYOURWORK. Free on iPhone and Android." doyourwork.today |

Notes
- The swear is never heard or spelled: only the horn and "#@%&!".
- Dares need each person to have their own phone, so they come in as the bonus beat, after the one-tablet setup.
- Store-listing rule: on the App Store page itself, keep the words household and family, not "for kids". Everywhere else this ad can play as is.
